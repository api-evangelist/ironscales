# IRONSCALES

> IRONSCALES is an AI-powered, API-based email security platform that protects 17,000+ organizations from phishing, business email compromise (BEC), account takeover, and other advanced email threats. The platform combines adaptive AI detection, computer vision, automated remediation across all affected mailboxes, human risk management, and a crowdsourced threat intelligence network — deployed in minutes via API with no MX record changes.

IRONSCALES was named a Visionary in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Email Security Platforms (2024, 2025) and a Leader & Outperformer in the GigaOm Anti-Phishing Radar (2025).
