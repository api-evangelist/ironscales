If you run email security on Microsoft 365 E3, you woke up to an upgrade you didn't ask for. As of July 1, Microsoft folds Defender for Office 365 Plan 1 into Microsoft 365 E3 and Office 365 E3 , and it lands in tenants through August 1. Safe Links, Safe Attachments, and a stronger anti-phishing engine are switching on, in a lot of cases before anyone on your team touches a setting.
